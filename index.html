Thailand Tourist Visa (TR)

Single and multiple entry options for holidays, family visits and short-term stays

Visa Overview
  • Visa type: TR — Tourist Visa (Non-Immigrant)
  • Stay permitted: 60 days per entry, extendable by 30 days at a local immigration office
  • Entry options: Single entry or multiple entry
  • Validity from issue: 3 months (single entry) or 6–12 months (multiple entry)
  • Application location: Thai embassy or consulate in your home country
Who Is It For?
  • Holidaymakers and leisure travellers visiting Thailand
  • Those visiting family or friends for an extended stay
  • Travellers who require more than the standard 30-day visa exemption
  • Anyone planning multiple trips to Thailand within a year (multiple entry)
Key Documents Required
  • Valid passport with at least 18 months remaining validity
  • Completed Thai visa application form
  • Recent passport-sized photographs
  • Proof of onward travel (return or onward flight booking)
  • Proof of accommodation in Thailand
  • Evidence of sufficient funds (bank statement — typically 20,000 THB or equivalent)
Extension Information
  • Single-entry tourist visas can be extended by 30 days at an immigration office in Thailand
  • Extension fee: 1,900 THB payable to Thai Immigration
  • Maximum total stay: 90 days per entry with extension
  • Further extensions require leaving the country or changing visa category
Please note: Visa requirements are subject to change by Thai immigration authorities. Our team always verifies current requirements before submitting your application.

Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)

Thailand's dedicated visa for digital nomads, remote workers and long-stay travellers

Visa Overview
  • Visa type: DTV — Destination Thailand Visa
  • Stay permitted: 180 days per entry (extendable by a further 180 days in-country)
  • Entry type: Multiple entry
  • Visa validity: 5 years from date of issue
  • Government fee: 10,000 THB (approx.)
  • Application location: Thai embassy or consulate abroad
Who Is It For?
  • Digital nomads and remote workers earning income from abroad
  • Freelancers, consultants and independent contractors
  • Participants in Thai government soft power activities (Muay Thai, Thai cooking, traditional arts)
  • Long-stay travellers who spend extended periods in Thailand
  • Individuals attending seminars, exhibitions or training programmes
Key Eligibility Requirements
  • Minimum funds: 500,000 THB (approx. USD 14,000) in a bank account
  • Evidence of remote work, freelance contracts or self-employment
  • Valid passport with sufficient remaining validity
  • Health insurance with minimum 40,000 THB outpatient / 400,000 THB inpatient coverage
  • No intention to work for a Thai-based employer (foreign-sourced income only)
Key Benefits
  • No Thai work permit required for work performed for overseas employers or clients
  • 5-year validity allows multiple extended stays without repeated embassy visits
  • Each entry allows up to 180 days, extendable to 360 days in-country
  • Covers soft power activities such as Muay Thai training and culinary courses
  • Spouse and dependent children may be eligible for accompanying visas
Important: The DTV does not permit employment with a Thai company or organisation. All income must be derived from foreign sources. Our advisors will confirm your eligibility before proceeding.

Work & Business Visa (Non-B)

Non-Immigrant B visa and work permit assistance for employees and business owners

Visa Overview
  • Visa type: Non-Immigrant B (Business / Work)
  • Initial stay: 90 days per entry
  • Extension: Annual renewal (1 year) once a work permit is obtained
  • Entry type: Single entry (multiple entry available)
  • Linked requirement: Work permit required to legally work in Thailand
  • Application location: Thai embassy or consulate abroad
Who Is It For?
  • Foreign nationals employed by a Thai registered company
  • Business owners and directors of Thai limited companies
  • Individuals conducting business meetings, negotiations or trade in Thailand
  • BOI-promoted company employees
Key Documents Required
  • Letter of employment or invitation from a Thai company
  • Company registration documents (DBD certificate, VAT, shareholder list)
  • Copies of corporate income tax returns (Por Ngor Dor 50)
  • Employee list with social security contributions
  • Valid passport with at least 18 months remaining
  • Educational qualifications relevant to the position
Work Permit Process
  • Work permit applications are submitted to the Thai Department of Employment
  • Processing time: approximately 5–7 business days once documents are complete
  • Annual renewal required alongside visa extension
  • One work permit per employer — changes of employer require a new application
Please note: Thai law requires a minimum ratio of Thai to foreign employees (typically 4:1) for work permit eligibility. We advise on company compliance as part of our service.

Student & Education Visa (ED)

Non-Immigrant ED visa for language schools, universities and certified programmes

Visa Overview
  • Visa type: Non-Immigrant ED (Education)
  • Initial stay: 90 days, extendable annually
  • Extension: Renewable in-country for the duration of study
  • 90-day reporting: Required by Thai immigration law throughout your stay
  • Application location: Thai embassy or consulate abroad
Who Is It For?
  • Students enrolled at accredited Thai universities or colleges
  • Those attending Thai language courses at licensed language schools
  • Students participating in certified Muay Thai, yoga or wellness training
  • Individuals enrolled in licensed vocational programmes
Key Documents Required
  • Acceptance or enrolment letter from a Thai-government approved institution
  • Valid passport with at least 18 months remaining validity
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Evidence of financial means to support yourself during study
  • Completed Thai visa application form
90-Day Reporting
  • All visa holders in Thailand for more than 90 consecutive days must report to immigration
  • Can be completed online, by post, or in person at an immigration office
  • Failure to report incurs a fine of 2,000 THB (up to 5,000 THB if apprehended)
  • We handle 90-day reporting on your behalf as part of our ongoing support service
Please note: The ED visa does not permit employment in Thailand. The educational institution must hold a valid licence issued by the Thai Ministry of Education.

Visa Renewals & Extensions

In-country visa renewals and extensions handled end-to-end on your behalf

What We Handle
  • Tourist visa (TR) 30-day extensions at Thai immigration offices
  • Annual Non-Immigrant B (work visa) renewals
  • Non-Immigrant ED (student) annual renewals
  • DTV in-country 180-day extensions
  • Retirement visa (Non-O) renewals for those aged 50 and over
The Process
  • We assess your current visa status and advise on the correct renewal route
  • We prepare your complete document package in advance of the immigration appointment
  • We attend the immigration office on your behalf where permitted, or accompany you
  • We handle payment of government extension fees (1,900 THB for tourist extensions)
  • We notify you immediately upon confirmation of your renewed visa
Important Timing
  • Extensions should be applied for no more than 30 days before your current permission expires
  • Overstaying your visa is subject to a fine of 500 THB per day (maximum 20,000 THB)
  • Overstay exceeding 90 days may result in a re-entry ban — contact us immediately if you are at risk
  • We recommend beginning the process at least 2 weeks before your visa expiry
Reminder: Staying in Thailand beyond your permitted period is a criminal offence under the Immigration Act B.E. 2522. If your visa is approaching expiry, contact us as soon as possible.
